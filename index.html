For most of the people we build for, a smartphone is shared between five family members, data costs a day's wage, and the nearest catechist is two bus rides away. Digital Faith for All exists to close that distance — turning the Church's oldest texts and practices into something that opens in under two seconds on a 3G connection.

Why we build this

Our mission

Faith formation has always assumed a few things: a Bible on the shelf, a catechist nearby, a parish that can afford to print. For a large share of the world's Catholics — rural, low-income, sharing one phone between a household — none of that holds. Digital Faith for All starts from the opposite assumption: build for the person with the least, and the rest of the Church can use it too.

How we decide what to build

Every resource is reviewed for doctrinal accuracy by parish clergy before release, then tested with the people it's actually for — a catechist in a two-room parish, a grandmother on a shared family phone, a teenager who prays mostly by audio during a commute.

Get involved

We're always looking for translators, catechists willing to pilot new lessons, and parishes who can tell us what's missing. If that's you, reach out — the next language we add might be the one you speak.
